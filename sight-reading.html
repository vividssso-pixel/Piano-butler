Common questions

Is the sight-reading generator free?

Yes — it's free to use, with no login or account required. Generate as many excerpts as you like.

Does it match the AMEB exam format?

Excerpts are written to reflect the general difficulty and style a student is expected to sight-read at each AMEB grade — they're for practice, not official AMEB exam material.

Why does it sometimes take a moment to load?

The generator runs on its own server, which can take a few extra seconds to wake up if it's been idle. A loading screen will show progress and take you straight to your excerpt once it's ready.

Can I use it for ABRSM or Trinity sight-reading practice?

The generator is built specifically around the AMEB syllabus's grading, but the reading practice itself is useful regardless of which board a student is preparing for.

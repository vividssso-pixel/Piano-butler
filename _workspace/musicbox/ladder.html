Your Accompaniment Ladder

Three books, nine steps. The right hand plays the tune; the left hand learns, one step at a time, to make it sound beautiful. At the end of every book, Twinkle comes back, so you can hear how far your left hand has grown.

About one song a week. Each book takes about five months: 18 songs, then Twinkle.

Decided along the way

  1. Sound before symbol: say and clap, then listen and sing, then play.
  2. Chords grow one note a stage, each stage held for two or three songs: the root (lessons 5 to 7), the two ends of the hand (9 to 11), the whole chord (12 to 15). One line of explanation a stage.
  3. Chords shown as a lead sheet: letters above the tune, only when the left hand plays below it. The bass clef waits for lesson 8.
  4. Moving a song to a new key comes later, white keys only at first; the five-finger rule after the hand has felt it.
  5. No teacher part in the book; the piano duet lives in the play-along.
  6. Classical themes get short English words of our own; melodies are checked against public scores before they are set.

Lesson numbers and ranges may move once each song is set. Files: _workspace/songbook/ on the Mac.

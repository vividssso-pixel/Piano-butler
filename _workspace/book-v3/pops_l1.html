Example 1
Left hand on C D E F G: which finger plays G?
1Little finger (5) on C.
2Walk up toward the thumb: 4, 3, 2.
3The last key, G: which finger is left?
G is the thumb, 1. From C up, the left hand plays 5 4 3 2 1: the mirror of the right.

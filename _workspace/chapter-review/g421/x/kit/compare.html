Victorian, kept minimal

The same pages, words and drawings -- only the dress changes. Shown on chapter 5 (and two pages from chapters 1 and 4): each sheet that follows puts now on the left and the Victorian sample on the right.

What changes

  • Paper: a fine frame with cut-in corners; the page number sits on the frame.
  • Type: small labels in spaced capitals; subheads and numbers in Playfair italic; titles centred over a small diamond ornament; a drop capital on each chapter's first page; dotted leaders in the contents.
  • Shapes: square corners with a fine inner line on picture boxes; the workbook's labels with pointed ends; double rules on the PLAY box, the REMEMBER band and the five checks.
  • Colour, by job (right).

What stays

Every page's layout and size, the drawings, Inter for reading (easy for children), white paper (cheap to print at home). Checked: no page overflows in any Lesson Book, Workbook or Teacher copy, chapters 1-5.

Colour, by job

Navy -- the ink. Words, lines, notes, the boxes to write in.

Coral -- the point. Look here: the example, the key word, YOUR TURN. Used less than now.

Green -- the check. Tick boxes, Got it!, Checked, the Teacher copy's answers. (The app's "correct" green.)

Cream -- the picture box. As now.

Why AMEB pink is riskier than ABRSM navy / Trinity green were: navy and bottle-green don't exist anywhere else in the site's color system, so they were free to claim. Pink is already taken — it's the Romantic-era tag color (#be185d) on every single board's pieces, AMEB included. Any pink accent for "this is an AMEB piece" will sit right next to "this is a Romantic-era piece" on a lot of cards. Option 2 (deep berry) is the safer version if pink is important to you; Option 3 (stay ink) sidesteps the collision entirely and matches what was decided in the original brand pass — AMEB as the neutral flagship, ABRSM/Trinity as the two colored boards.
Scope if we go ahead with the background unification: this touches the <style> block and the header/tab/card markup on every inner page — 9 AMEB grade pages + 11 ABRSM pages + 12 Trinity pages, 32 files total, not just a color swap this time. Structurally bigger than last time's edit, so worth confirming the direction here before I start.

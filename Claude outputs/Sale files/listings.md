## Store blurb (top of the shop page)
Clear, friendly piano theory, one topic at a time. Each part has the Handbook pages (the idea, with pictures and examples) and the Workbook pages (exercises with the first answer done, a review and the answers). Read the whole Handbook free online, with every chapter read aloud, at thepianobutler.com/books/read/. Teachers may copy single Workbook pages for their own students.

## Each product page
**What you get:** the Handbook part as a printable PDF and its Workbook pages with answers.
**Who it is for:** students from the first lesson to Grade 8, parents helping at home, adults coming back to the piano, and teachers.
**How it works:** read the idea in the Handbook (or listen to it free online), then write in the Workbook. The first answer of most exercises is done, so you can see what to do. Every part ends with a review and the answers.
**Independent:** not affiliated with any examination board. Music examples are public domain.

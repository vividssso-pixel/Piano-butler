## Store blurb (top of the shop page)
Clear, friendly music theory for pianists, one topic at a time. Every chapter starts with one page that holds all its facts, with a picture; the Workbook gives you pages to write, with the first answer done and the answers at the back. Read the whole Handbook free online, with every chapter read aloud and the worked examples explained step by step, at thepianobutler.com/books/read/. For beginners through to Grade 8 (AMEB, ABRSM and Trinity students alike), adults coming back to the piano, and teachers.

## Each product page
**What you get:** the Handbook part as a printable PDF and its Workbook pages with answers.
**Who it is for:** piano students from the very first lesson up to Grade 8, parents helping at home, adults coming back to the piano, and teachers.
**How it works:** read the chapter's page in the Handbook (or listen to it free online, with the worked examples), then write in the Workbook. The first answer of most exercises is done, so you can see what to do. Every part ends with a review, a progress page and the answers.
**Independent:** not affiliated with any examination board.

The musical alphabet

Every white key has a name: a letter from A to G. Learn where each one lives, then make the names come fast, until any key tells you its name at a glance.

Written by Clara Sohyun Park · Updated 13 October 2026

From the teacher

The note names themselves rarely hold a student up for long. The part children find harder is skipping a letter: C, E, G, jumping over one letter each time.

And one thing matters as much as finding the letters on the keys: saying the alphabet backwards in your head, without looking at the keyboard pattern. I have students practise that on its own, away from the piano.

A to G on the keys

Music uses the first seven letters of the alphabet: A B C D E F G. The lowest key on the piano is an A. From there the white keys go up A to G, then A again.

{{svg:a-to-g}}
  • C D E live in the small house, the one with two black keys.
  • F G A B live in the big house, the one with three black keys.
  • C and F are the two left doors. Find them first and walk from there.

The letter wheel and the octave

After G comes A again, like the days of the week. There is no H.

From one C to the next is an octave: 8 white keys, counting both Cs. oct means eight, like an octopus's eight legs. Same name, higher voice.

{{svg:wheel}}

Forwards up, backwards down

Going up (right), the letters go forwards: C D E F G A B C. Going down (left), they go backwards: C B A G F E D C. Backwards is harder, especially the turn from A to G.

{{svg:forwards-backwards}}
Teacher's tip

Practise the backwards alphabet away from the piano too: in the car, in the bath, on the way to school. Start from G, then from E, then from any letter, until backwards comes as quickly as forwards.

Name any key at a glance

Walking up from C every time is slow. Look at who lives there.

C: Clara, a little princess, at the small house's left door. D: her Dog, in his doghouse between the two black keys. E: the Exit, the right door.

F: the big house's Front door. G: Grandma, who lives inside and loves apples. A: her Apple garden, right next to her. B: the Back door.

{{svg:who-lives}}

Each name starts with its key's letter and sits in its key's place. Don't count from C: ask who lives there.

Skip a letter

A step goes to the very next letter. A skip jumps over one letter, one white key: C, (D), E, (F), G. Skip round, C E G B D F A and back to C, and the lines draw a seven-pointed star.

{{svg:skip}}

At first, whisper the skipped letter, C (d) E (f) G. Then drop the whisper.

Play by letter names

Right-hand fingers 2, 3 and 4 sit on C, D and E in the small house. Read each letter, see its place, play. Read the letters, not the finger numbers. Then the left hand, one small house lower.

{{svg:play-cde}}
  1. Read and playRight hand 2 3 4 on C D E, saying the letters. One circle is one steady beat.
    {{beats:C D E - | E D C - | C E D C | E - C -}}

Spell and play: a word made only of music letters is a tiny tune. Try BAG, CAFE, FACE, BEAD and EGG, saying each letter.

My five checks

Show each one at the piano, or say it out loud.

On the keyboard

  • I can find A to G on the keys
  • I can name any white key at a glance
  • I can read letters and play them: C D E, BAG, CAFE

Out loud

  • I can say the letters forwards and backwards, from any letter
  • I can skip up and down: C E G B D F A

Common questions

What are the note names on the piano?

The white keys use the first seven letters of the alphabet: A B C D E F G. After G comes A again, so the seven names repeat all the way up the piano. There is no H.

How do you remember the names of the piano keys?

Use the black keys. C D E sit around the two black keys, F G A B around the three. C is just left of the two, F just left of the three. D sits between the two, G and A between the three.

What is an octave on the piano?

The distance from one key to the next key with the same letter, like C up to the next C: 8 white keys, counting both ends. oct means eight.

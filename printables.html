Free piano printables

Cut-outs, card games and worksheet pages for piano lessons, made by a piano teacher for her own students. Print them on A4, cut them out, and play. Free, with no sign-up.

How to print
  • Each button opens the pages ready to print. Press Print / Save as PDF there.
  • Print on A4 at 100% (actual size) so the pieces fit together.
  • Flashcards are two-sided: print the fronts, then the backs on the other side, flipping on the long edge.
  • Card or thick paper makes the cut-outs last longer.

The Piano Butler’s house

Piano lessons for young beginners, one chapter at a time. Every floor of the house is a stage and every room is a chapter: your butler meets you in the hall, and you go up, floor by floor.

Written by Clara Sohyun Park · Updated 10 October 2026

Rooms in dark ink have a guide page. The grey rooms are still being written: one chapter at a time, each one tried at the piano first.

Open rooms

1

Posture and hand shape

How to sit, six arm warm-ups, a round hand, finger numbers and first finger walks. With a free worksheet.

 2

Keyboard patterns

The black-key houses, the white keys that belong to each one, high and low, middle C and first legato playing on the black keys. With a free worksheet.

 3

The musical alphabet

The seven letters A to G on the white keys, forwards and backwards, every key named at a glance, skipping a letter, and words spelled on the keys. With a free worksheet.

 4

Reading the staff

Lines and spaces, steps and skips, the Queen’s treble clef and the King’s bass clef, and the nine notes round middle C. With a free worksheet.

What each guide page has

  • The chapter in pictures, drawn the same way as the Lesson Book.
  • What students get wrong most, and what helps, from the teacher.
  • A free worksheet to print for your own students or your own children.
  • A lesson to try on screen, with pictures that move, in the Piano Butler app.

The Piano Butler’s house

Piano lessons for young beginners, one chapter at a time. Every floor of the house is a stage and every room is a chapter, from the front door up to the attic.

Written by Clara Sohyun Park · Updated 8 October 2026

Rooms in dark ink have a guide page. The grey rooms are still being written: one chapter at a time, each one tried at the piano first.

Posture and hand shape

Keyboard patterns

The black-key houses, the white keys that belong to each one, the seven letter names, middle C and a first black-key tune. With a free worksheet.

What each guide page has

  • The chapter in pictures, drawn the same way as the Lesson Book.
  • What students get wrong most, and what helps, from the teacher.
  • A free worksheet to print for your own students or your own children.
  • A lesson to try on screen, with pictures that move, in the Piano Butler app.
